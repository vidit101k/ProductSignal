# ProductSignal — Master Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview
- Product name: ProductSignal
- Product type: E-Commerce Product Growth & Decision Intelligence System
- Product description: A data-driven decision-support system that analyzes e-commerce performance, identifies business opportunities, and translates insights into actionable product recommendations.
- Tagline: Turn product data into measurable business decisions.

## 2. Problem Statement
Describe the business problem ProductSignal addresses:
- Limited visibility into the e-commerce purchase funnel.
- Difficulty identifying conversion bottlenecks.
- Limited understanding of channel and customer performance.
- Difficulty translating raw analytics into product decisions.

## 3. Product Vision
Help businesses make better product and growth decisions by connecting data analysis, business insights, and AI-assisted recommendations.

## 4. Target Users
- Product Managers
- E-commerce Business Managers
- Growth and Marketing Teams
- Data Analysts

## 5. Product Objectives
- Understand overall business and revenue performance.
- Identify funnel conversion bottlenecks.
- Evaluate channel and customer performance.
- Identify opportunities for product and revenue improvement.
- Support evidence-based decisions using analytics and AI.

## 6. Product Scope

### In Scope
- E-commerce data cleaning and analysis.
- Business KPI calculations.
- Interactive Streamlit dashboard.
- Funnel and conversion analysis.
- Channel and customer insights.
- Product opportunity identification.
- A/B testing design.
- AI-assisted business analysis.
- Product recommendations and PRDs.

### Out of Scope
- Live e-commerce platform integration.
- Real-time production experimentation.
- Actual payment processing.
- Verified customer behavior prediction.
- Profitability and ROI measurement without cost data.

## 7. Core Product Features
1. Business Performance Overview
2. Funnel Analysis
3. Channel Performance
4. Customer Insights
5. Product Decision Center
6. A/B Testing
7. AI Insights

## 8. Key Product Metrics
- Net Revenue
- Completed Orders
- Gross Average Order Value
- Net Revenue per Order
- View-to-Cart Conversion
- Cart-to-Checkout Conversion
- Checkout-to-Purchase Conversion
- Overall Purchase Conversion
- Repeat Purchase Rate

## 9. Product Initiatives
- P1: Product Page Conversion Optimization
- P2: Checkout Optimization
- P3: Discount Strategy Optimization

Refer to the individual initiative PRDs for detailed requirements, hypotheses, success metrics, and recommendations.

## 10. AI Integration
AI is used to interpret calculated business metrics and generate structured business findings, hypotheses, and recommendations.

Python performs the metric calculations; the AI layer supports interpretation and decision-making.

## 11. Assumptions and Limitations
- The project uses a synthetic educational e-commerce dataset.
- Observed relationships do not establish causality.
- A/B testing results are not from a completed live experiment.
- Revenue improvement estimates are scenario-based.
- CAC, advertising spend, contribution margin, and ROI cannot be reliably evaluated without additional data.
- AI-generated recommendations require human review.

## 12. Roadmap
- Phase 1: Data cleaning and validation.
- Phase 2: Business and funnel analysis.
- Phase 3: Interactive dashboard development.
- Phase 4: Product opportunity identification and PRD development.
- Phase 5: AI-assisted business analysis.
- Phase 6: Final validation and documentation.
